🎯 What Boston Beer Must Do to Increase Category Share

Walmart 1st party data reveals clear seasonal gaps where BBC brands under-pace against their respective category benchmarks. Closing these gaps is the primary driver of category share growth at Walmart.

Twisted Tea · Hard Beverage Category

Close the Spring & Summer Gap

+21.34%
Category YoY growth

The Problem

  • Twisted Tea under-paced the Hard Beverage category in Spring (March–May)
  • March–May accounts for 25% of all annual category sales — a prime missed window
  • Under-paced further in key summer months, then dropped sharply in Fall

What to Do

  • Launch display campaigns by March to intercept shoppers before category peak
  • Increase investment in May–July to match category momentum
  • Maintain Fall presence through October to prevent the drop-off
  • Add CTV and Audio to capture Gen-Z during peak summer streaming season

Angry Orchard · Craft Beer Category

Re-Engage Summer Shoppers & Sustain Fall Momentum

-14.95%
Angry Orchard YoY

The Problem

  • Angry Orchard lagged behind the Craft Beer category in June and July — the category's peak sales months
  • Sales are -14.95% YoY — the steepest decline across BBC brands
  • WMC activation in Oct/Nov did outpace the category — proving WMC data works for this brand

What to Do

  • Launch a dedicated summer campaign by June to close the category gap during peak months
  • Leverage lapsed buyer audiences to win back shoppers lost in the YoY decline
  • Extend the successful Oct/Nov WMC strategy to cover June–July
  • Use Craft Beer audience taxonomy to conquest switchers moving to competing craft brands
Sun Cruiser & Truly · Hard Beverage Category

Front-Load Spring Investment to Capture Seasonal Demand

Flat
Sun Cruiser YoY

The Problem

  • Sun Cruiser under-paces against the Hard Beverage category — despite being a breakout brand
  • Truly saw peak sales in Sept/Oct, but is missing April–July — the prime amplification window
  • Both brands launched late in FY25 ($70K and $65K respectively) — undersized investment

What to Do

  • April–July is the prime investment window to drive sales and brand awareness for both brands
  • Increase Sun Cruiser and Truly campaign budgets above $150K so they qualify for Walmart Sales Lift and Brand Lift reporting in FY26.
  • Target "In-Market Hard Beverage" and "Hard Seltzer" category audiences for Sun Cruiser
  • Use "Healthy Living" and "Carb Conscious" personas for Truly to reach better-for-you shoppers
